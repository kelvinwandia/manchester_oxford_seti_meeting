Getting here

Manchester Piccadilly station is about a 20-minute walk or short bus ride along Oxford Road. Manchester Airport has direct trains to Piccadilly.

Accommodation

We hope to cover accomodation for attendees travelling from Oxford. Other attendees should book their own; there are many hotels near Oxford Road and the city centre.
