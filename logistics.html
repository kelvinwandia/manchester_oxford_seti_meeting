Getting here

Manchester Piccadilly station is about a 20-minute walk or short bus ride from the Schuster Building along Oxford Road. Manchester Airport has direct trains to Piccadilly.

Jodrell Bank is about 25 miles south of Manchester. Details of transport for day 2 will follow.

Accommodation

Attendees will stay at the Hyatt Regency Manchester, 55 Booth Street West, M15 6PQ. It's about a 7-minute walk from the Schuster Building. We hope to cover accommodation for attendees travelling from Oxford. Other attendees should book their own.
