Meeting Overview

The fist joint SETI meeting between researchers at the University of Manchester and the University of Oxford will be the hosted by Jodrell Bank Centre for Astrophysics, The University of Manchester, United Kingdom, on 2-3 March 2027.

The two-day meeting will take place in the Schuster Building at the Department of Physics and Astronomy.

The meeting will bring together members of the SETI community at the two universities to share recent results, new methods and instrumentation, and consider priorities and challanges facing the field over the coming decade. Across the two days, we expect contributed talks, invited presentations and an open discussion on the future of SETI.
