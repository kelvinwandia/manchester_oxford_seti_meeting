Meeting Overview

The SETI Meeting 2027 will be hosted by Jodrell Bank Centre for Astrophysics, The University of Manchester, United Kingdom, in early March 2027 (dates to be confirmed).

The meeting will take place in the Department of Physics and Astronomy’s Schuster Building.

This two-day meeting brings together researchers working on the search for extraterrestrial intelligence and technosignatures to share recent results, new methods and instrumentation, and to look ahead to the next decade of the field. The programme includes contributed talks, two invited presentations and an open discussion on the future of SETI.

List of Topics

  • Instrumentation and next-generation facilities, including SKA
  • The future of SETI
